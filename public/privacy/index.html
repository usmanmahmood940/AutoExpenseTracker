Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

NovaSpend ("we", "us", or "our") helps you track personal spending by processing bank SMS alerts you choose to forward through iOS Shortcuts. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and authentication identifiers when you create an account (via Firebase Authentication).
  • Transaction data: SMS text, parsed merchant, amount, date, bank, category, and related metadata you send through the ingest webhook.
  • Device information: push notification tokens if you enable alerts, and optional biometric lock preferences stored on your device.
  • Usage data: basic diagnostics such as sync timestamps and error logs needed to operate the service.

How we use information

  • Provide transaction tracking, search, insights, and exports.
  • Parse and categorize spending from SMS content you submit.
  • Authenticate your account and keep the service secure.
  • Send account-related emails (verification, password reset).
  • Improve parsing accuracy and product reliability.

How data is processed

When you configure iOS Shortcuts, SMS content is sent to our API hosted on Google Cloud Run and stored in our database (PostgreSQL). Authentication is handled by Firebase. We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention

We retain account and transaction data while your account is active. You may delete individual transactions in the app or delete your account from Settings. When you delete your account, we remove associated personal data from our systems, subject to reasonable backup retention periods.

Sharing

We use infrastructure and service providers (for example Google Cloud, Firebase, and email delivery providers) only to operate NovaSpend. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users and the service.

Security

We use HTTPS, authenticated API access, and industry-standard cloud security practices. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

  • Stop sending SMS data by disabling or removing your Shortcut.
  • Edit or delete transactions in the app.
  • Export your data as CSV from Settings.
  • Delete your account from Settings.
  • Contact us to ask questions about your data.

Children

NovaSpend is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the effective date above when we do. Continued use of NovaSpend after changes means you accept the updated policy.
